## Voice language normalization and per-agent audio controls

The comments standardize `ai_tts_voice.languages` and add voice language, volume, rate and pitch fields to agents and templates if absent. Dynamic-DDL comments mean `tts_language` = voice language, `tts_volume` = TTS volume, `tts_rate` = TTS speaking rate, `tts_pitch` = TTS pitch.

The exact Chinese source/target strings in lines 4–12 are compatibility data for normalization:

- Chinese/Mandarin, Northeastern and Tianjin speech, Beijing/Qingdao/Henan/Guangxi/Sichuan/Taiwan/Changsha accents, Liaoning and Shaanxi labels normalize to Mandarin.
- Chinese plus English, including mixed Chinese/English, American English combinations and Northeastern/Beijing mixed speech, normalize to Mandarin plus English.
- British/American/Australian English and the generic English label normalize to English.
- Japanese stays Japanese; Japanese plus abbreviated Spanish becomes Japanese plus the full Spanish label; Korean stays Korean.
- Cantonese and the Guangdong-accent label normalize to Cantonese. Cantonese/Chinese-Cantonese plus English combinations normalize to Cantonese plus English.

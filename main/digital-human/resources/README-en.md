## Hiyori Momose - PRO

The sample is a standard model made with Cubism 3.0. It demonstrates deformer
structure and parameter usage. Its shoulders use the Glue feature.

The notice says individuals and small businesses may use the assets commercially
if they accept the license agreement. Medium and large enterprises are limited
to non-public internal evaluation. Users must check the license type and Live2D
original-character provisions of the agreement for freely supplied materials,
and accept the usage conditions for Live2D Cubism sample models. The notice
points to <https://www.live2d.com/zh-CHS/download/sample-data/> for license details.

Credits: illustration by Kani Biimu; modeling by Live2D.

Contents: a `.cmo3` model including physics settings, a `.can3` animation, and
runtime assets: `.moc3` model data, `.motion3.json` motions, `.model3.json` model
settings, `.physics3.json` physics, `.pose3.json` poses and `.cdi3.json` display
metadata.

## Natori Jin - PRO

This sample demonstrates advanced production techniques such as arm switching,
commonly used in commercial games and apps. The character was designed by Makoto
Senzaki. Fades switch between arm parts; the sample demonstrates richer animation
through part switching. Cubism3 Viewer (for OW) can preview runtime animations
and help explain the data structures used by the full workflow.

The same usage conditions described in the Hiyori notice above apply in this
notice: commercial use for individuals/small businesses subject to agreement,
non-public internal evaluation only for medium/large enterprises, and acceptance
of the referenced license and sample-model conditions. Consult the original
notice and vendor terms rather than treating this summary as permission.

Credits: illustration by Makoto Senzaki (<http://senzakimakoto.com/>), voice by
Yuki Ono (<https://web.onoyuki.com/>), modeling by Live2D. Voice data was a limited
release; its distribution ended June 4, 2018.

Contents: a `.cmo3` model including physics settings, expression and base `.can3`
animations, and runtime assets: `.moc3` model data, `.exp3.json` expressions,
`.motion3.json` motions, `.model3.json` settings, `.pose3.json` poses,
`.physics3.json` physics and `.cdi3.json` display metadata.

History: `natori_pro_t06.cmo3`, `natori_pro_exp_t03.can3`, and
`natori_pro_motions_t03.can3` were published June 10, 2021. The history repeats
that voice-data distribution ended June 4, 2018.
